# Festive Sale 2026

## Offer Period

The Festive Sale runs from 10 October to 15 November 2026 online and in all NovaMart stores in India.

## Discounts

Up to 25% off televisions and home appliances, and up to 15% off laptops and mobiles. Partner bank cards get an extra 10% instant discount of up to ₹3,000 per card.

## Returns on Sale Items

Items bought during the Festive Sale follow the standard Online Return Policy. Clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## Exchange Offer

Exchange an old phone, TV or appliance in working condition for an extra discount, assessed at pickup.

## Price Protection

Gold and Platinum members who buy an item that drops in price within 7 days of purchase during the sale receive the difference as NovaMart Wallet credit.
