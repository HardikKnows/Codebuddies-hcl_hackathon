# Online Return Policy

This policy covers products bought on the NovaMart website and app. Version 2026.2 replaces version 2026.1 from 1 July 2026.

## Return Window

Most products bought online can be returned within 10 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are unused, undamaged and in their original packaging with all accessories, manuals and free gifts. Gold and Platinum members get an extended 15-day return window on non-electronics.

## Opened Electronics

Opened mobile phones, laptops, tablets and wearables can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if the device works and has no physical or liquid damage, it is signed out of all accounts and factory reset, and the original box, charger, cables and documentation are included.

A NovaMart technician inspects every opened device before the refund is approved. Opened devices with damage caused after delivery are not eligible for return, but may still be covered by warranty.

## Damaged or Defective on Arrival

If a product arrives damaged (for example a cracked screen or a dented appliance) or does not work, report it within 48 hours of delivery through the app or a support ticket, with photos of the product and the packaging. NovaMart arranges a free pickup and sends a replacement, or issues a full refund if no replacement is available. Damage-on-arrival claims are not counted against the return window.

## Discounted and Sale Items

Products bought at a discount, including during sales and promotions, follow the same return rules as full-price products. Items marked "Clearance" or "Final Sale" cannot be returned unless they are defective or damaged on arrival.

## Returning Online Purchases to a Store

Online purchases, including discounted items, can be returned at any NovaMart store in the same country. Bring the order number and the product in its original packaging. Large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines and TVs above 43 inches) must be returned through a scheduled home pickup instead of a store visit.

## Refund Timelines

Refunds are initiated within 2 business days after a return passes inspection. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear; UPI and NovaMart Wallet refunds take up to 3 business days. Refunds always go back to the original payment method.

## Non-returnable Items

Opened earphones and earbuds (for hygiene reasons), gift cards, software and activation codes, and personal care appliances with broken seals cannot be returned unless they are defective.
