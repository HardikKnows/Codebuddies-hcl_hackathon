# Delivery and Shipping Policy (India)

## Delivery Options

Standard delivery reaches most PIN codes in 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery arrives the next day in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai and Hyderabad for orders placed before 2 PM. Home delivery orders ship from the NovaMart Central Warehouse.

## Delivery Charges

Standard delivery is free for orders of ₹5,000 or more and costs ₹149 below that. Express delivery costs ₹299 per order. Express delivery is free for Gold and Platinum members, and Silver members pay ₹149 per express order (see Membership Benefits).

## In-Store Pickup

Pickup orders are ready within 2 hours when the item is in stock at the chosen store. Bring the order confirmation and a photo ID. Orders not collected within 3 days are cancelled and refunded automatically.

## Large Appliances

Refrigerators, washing machines and TVs above 43 inches are delivered on a scheduled slot. Installation is free and is scheduled within 48 hours of delivery.

## Tracking and Delays

Track every order from My Orders. If an order is late by more than 2 business days, contact support and NovaMart will refund the delivery charge.
