UAE DELIVERY POLICY

DELIVERY OPTIONS
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days anywhere in the UAE. Same-day delivery is available in Dubai and Abu Dhabi for orders placed before 12 PM.

DELIVERY CHARGES
Standard delivery is free for orders above AED 200. Same-day delivery costs AED 25 per order and is free for Platinum members.

RETURNS PICKUP
Returns in the UAE are collected from your address within 2 business days at no charge.
