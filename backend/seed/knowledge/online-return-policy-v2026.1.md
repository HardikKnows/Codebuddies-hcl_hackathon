# Online Return Policy

Version 2026.1, superseded by version 2026.2 on 1 July 2026.

## Return Window

Most products bought online can be returned within 10 days of delivery for a full refund if they are unused and in their original packaging.

## Opened Electronics

Opened mobile phones, laptops and tablets can be returned within 10 days of delivery if the device is undamaged and factory reset.

## Discounted and Sale Items

Discounted items can only be returned through an online return request. They cannot be returned to a physical store.

## Refund Timelines

Refunds are initiated within 5 business days after the return passes inspection.
